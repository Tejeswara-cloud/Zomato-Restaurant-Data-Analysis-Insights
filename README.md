# Zomato Restaurant Data Analysis & Insights

## 📊 Project Overview

This project performs exploratory data analysis (EDA) on the Zomato restaurant dataset to uncover meaningful insights into restaurant ratings, cuisines, locations, pricing, customer preferences, and other factors influencing restaurant performance.

The analysis focuses on transforming raw restaurant data into actionable business insights using Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and WordCloud.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Clean and preprocess the Zomato restaurant dataset.
* Handle missing values and inconsistent text fields.
* Analyze restaurant ratings and voting patterns.
* Identify popular and highly rated cuisines.
* Discover restaurant location hotspots.
* Analyze the relationship between restaurant pricing and ratings.
* Study factors such as online ordering and table booking.
* Create visualizations including bar charts, scatter plots, correlation heatmaps, and word clouds.
* Generate business recommendations that can support restaurant partnerships and customer engagement strategies.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* **Python**
* **Pandas** – Data cleaning and analysis
* **NumPy** – Numerical operations
* **Matplotlib** – Data visualization
* **Seaborn** – Statistical visualization
* **WordCloud** – Cuisine and dish popularity visualization
* **Jupyter Notebook** – Analysis environment

## 📈 Key Analysis Areas

### Restaurant Ratings

Analyzed the distribution of restaurant ratings and identified patterns in restaurant performance.

### Cuisine Analysis

Identified the most popular cuisines and compared cuisines based on their average restaurant ratings.

### Location Analysis

Analyzed restaurant concentration across locations to identify major restaurant hotspots.

### Price vs Rating

Explored whether restaurant pricing has a relationship with customer ratings.

### Customer Engagement

Analyzed votes, online ordering availability, table booking, and other restaurant attributes to understand their relationship with ratings.

### Popular Dishes

Used dish information to identify frequently mentioned dishes and created a word cloud to visualize food preferences.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the project provides recommendations for an Alfido Tech-style platform, including:

1. Develop partnerships with highly rated and popular restaurants.
2. Create location-based restaurant discovery and promotions.
3. Introduce budget, mid-range, and premium restaurant categories.
4. Create content around popular cuisines and dishes.
5. Develop a multi-factor restaurant recommendation system using ratings, votes, cuisine, price, and location.
